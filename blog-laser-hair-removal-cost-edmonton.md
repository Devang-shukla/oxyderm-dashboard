## 1. What Determines the Cost of Laser Hair Removal?

The price of laser hair removal is not arbitrary. It is governed by three primary factors:

### A. The Technology Used (Medical-Grade Laser vs. Generic IPL)
There is a fundamental difference between medical-grade lasers and low-cost aesthetic machines:
* **True Medical Lasers (Nd:YAG 1064nm & Alexandrite 755nm):** These machines cost between $80,000 and $150,000+. They deliver targeted wavelengths that bypass the skin's melanin and destroy the follicle root safely, regardless of skin tone.
* **Low-Cost Generic IPL Devices:** Many budget spas use broad-spectrum IPL systems. While effective for some fair skin types, IPL scatters energy across the skin surface. On medium to deep skin tones, this carries a high risk of burns, blisters, and post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH).

### B. Treatment Area Size
Pricing scales with the physical surface area and time required:
* **Small Areas:** Upper lip, chin, unibrow, sideburns (10–15 minute sessions).
* **Medium Areas:** Underarms, standard bikini, neck, shoulders (15–30 minute sessions).
* **Large Areas:** Full legs, full back, Brazilian, chest and stomach (45–60 minute sessions).

### C. Number of Sessions Required
Laser hair removal only targets hair during its active **Anagen growth phase**. At any given time, only 15% to 20% of your follicles are in this phase. For comprehensive hair reduction (80%–90%+ permanent reduction), most clients require **6 to 8 sessions**, spaced 4 to 8 weeks apart.

## 3. The Hidden Risks of "Bargain" Laser Clinics

When an ad promises "Unlimited Laser for $199," be cautious. Budget operators frequently cut costs by:
1. **Dialing Down Fluence (Energy):** Using underpowered settings so you never see lasting results, keeping you buying endless touch-up packages.
2. **Untrained Technicians:** Having staff with minimal clinical training operate high-energy devices.
3. **Skipping the Test Patch:** Treating skin immediately without verifying skin reaction, which is the #1 cause of laser burns in Edmonton.
