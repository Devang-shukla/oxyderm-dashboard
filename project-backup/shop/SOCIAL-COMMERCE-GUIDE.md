## 1. Google Merchant Center

**Feed Category:** Health & Beauty > Personal Care > Cosmetics > Skin Care

**Steps:**
1. Go to merchants.google.com → Add products manually or via feed
2. Business name: Oxyderm Laser Clinic
3. Country: Canada | Currency: CAD
4. Add product URL: https://devang-shukla.github.io/oxyderm-dashboard/shop/
5. Map 7 collections to GMC categories above
6. Add GBP services menu: Shop Skincare → link to /shop/

**GBP Product Posts (weekly):**
Post one product per week with photo, price, and shop link

## 2. Meta Commerce Manager

**Catalog name:** Oxyderm Skincare Store

**Steps:**
1. Go to business.facebook.com → Commerce Manager → Add Catalog
2. Catalog type: Ecommerce
3. Upload product feed (CSV) — see format below
4. Connect to Instagram + Facebook Page
5. Enable product tagging in Reels and carousels

**CSV Feed Format:**
id,title,description,availability,price,link,image_link,brand,google_product_category

## 3. TikTok Shop (Canada)

**Steps:**
1. Go to seller-ca.tiktok.com → Register as seller
2. Business category: Beauty & Personal Care
3. Add products linking to shop URL
4. Use product anchor links in post-treatment tutorial videos
5. Tag products in LHR and microneedling aftercare content
