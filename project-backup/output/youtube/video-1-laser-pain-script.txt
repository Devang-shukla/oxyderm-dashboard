I'm Hetisha from Oxyderm Laser Clinic here in Edmonton, and today I'm answering the number one question we hear at consultations: Does it hurt? But more importantly, I'll tell you WHAT it feels like, which areas hurt most, and how we make it way more comfortable than you think.

Let's get real.

First, the honest answer: Yes, laser hair removal involves some sensation. But here's the thing—it's not what you're imagining. Most clients describe it as a quick rubber band snap against your skin, followed by warmth. It lasts a fraction of a second per pulse, and we're moving fast.

The actual pain level? On a scale of 1 to 10, most people rate it between a 3 and 5—depending on the area. For context, waxing is usually a 6 or 7. Tattoos? 7 to 9. So laser is less painful than both.

But here's where it gets interesting: not all body areas feel the same.

Least Painful Areas: Legs are 2 to 3 out of 10. Large, flat surfaces with thicker skin. Most clients say this feels like tiny pinches. Arms are similar—smooth sailing.

Moderate areas: Underarms are 4 to 5 out of 10. Thinner skin, but it's fast—takes 2 to 3 minutes total. Back and chest for men are 3 to 4 out of 10. Lots of surface area, but coarse hair can make it more intense.

Most Sensitive: Bikini or Brazilian are 6 to 7 out of 10 for the first session, drops to 4 to 5 by session 3. Why? The first time you have the most hair. Less hair equals less sensation. Also, thinner skin and nerve endings. Face areas like upper lip and chin are 5 to 6 out of 10. Nerve-rich area. The good news? It's the smallest area, so it's over in under 5 minutes.

Now, here's what affects YOUR personal pain level:

Number one: Hair Coarseness. Coarse, thick hair like bikini or men's back hair absorbs more laser energy. That means more heat, more sensation. The upside? Coarse hair responds FASTER to laser. You get better results in fewer sessions.

Number two: Skin Tone. The laser targets melanin or pigment in your hair. If you have darker skin, we use a specialized Nd:YAG laser that's gentler but still effective. It feels slightly cooler than the lasers used on lighter skin.

Number three: Your Pain Tolerance. Some people are just more sensitive. That's okay! We have options.

Here's what we do to keep you comfortable:

First: Cooling Gel. We apply a cooling gel before and during treatment. It acts like a cold compress, numbing the skin slightly and protecting it from heat.

Second: Built-In Cooling Technology. Our laser has a sapphire cooling tip that chills your skin RIGHT before each pulse. You feel the cool before you feel the zap. Game-changer.

Third: Numbing Cream, which is optional. For super sensitive areas like Brazilian or upper lip, we offer a topical numbing cream. You apply it 30 minutes before your appointment, and it reduces sensation by about 50 percent. Most people don't need it after the first session because less hair equals less pain, and your skin adapts.

Fourth: We Work FAST. Our lasers cover large areas quickly. Underarms? 3 to 5 minutes. Full legs? 30 minutes. Brazilian? 10 to 15 minutes. Speed equals less time feeling anything.

And here's the secret nobody tells you: It gets easier every session.

Why? Because each treatment destroys 15 to 20 percent of your hair. By session 3 or 4, you have WAY less hair to target. Less hair equals less laser pulses equals less sensation. Clients always tell us, Session 1 was intense, but by session 4, I barely felt it.

Here's how to make your experience as smooth as possible:

Before Your Appointment: Shave 24 hours before. Shorter hair equals less heat absorption equals less sensation. Avoid caffeine 2 hours before. Caffeine makes your nerves more sensitive. Don't come on your period for bikini treatments. Hormones make you more sensitive to pain.

During Treatment: Take deep breaths. Sounds cheesy, but it works. Breathe in before a pulse, out during. Ask us to slow down. We can adjust the speed if you need breaks.

After Treatment: Apply aloe vera gel. Soothes any redness or heat. Avoid hot showers for 24 hours. Keep it lukewarm.
