Step One: The Consultation, which takes 15 to 20 minutes.

First, you're NOT getting microneedling on day one—at least, not without understanding what it does and whether it's right for you. A proper microneedling appointment starts with a consultation, and this is where you and your technician figure out YOUR goals.

You'll sit down with a licensed technician. They'll ask: What bothers you? Acne scars? Wrinkles? Large pores? Texture? What's your goal? Smooth texture completely, or just improve it? Have you had microneedling before? What's your skincare routine? Some products need to be paused before treatment.

Then, they'll examine your skin closely: What TYPE of scars do you have? Ice pick, boxcar, rolling—each responds differently. How deep are they? Do you have active acne? If yes, we treat that FIRST before microneedling. What's your skin tone? Microneedling is safe for all skin types, but darker skin needs extra sun-protection care.

This isn't a one-size-fits-all thing. Your skin is unique, so your treatment plan should be too.

At Oxyderm, we'll give you a realistic estimate: how many sessions YOU need, not a generic 4 to 6 sessions guess. Some clients with shallow scars are thrilled after 3 sessions. Others with deep scars need 7 to 8. We tell you upfront.

Step Three: Prep and Numbing, which takes 20 to 30 minutes.

Once you're ready to proceed, here's the prep:

First: Face Cleaning. We cleanse your face to remove makeup, oil, and dirt. Clean skin equals lower infection risk and better product absorption.

Second: Numbing Cream, which is NOT Optional. Here's the truth: microneedling without numbing cream would be VERY painful—7 to 9 out of 10. With numbing? It's 3 to 5 out of 10—totally tolerable.

We apply a thick layer of numbing cream to your face and let it sit for 20 to 30 minutes. You'll feel tingling as it kicks in, then your skin goes slightly numb.

Pro tip: Some clinics rush this step with only 10 to 15 minutes of numbing. That's not enough. We give it the full 20 to 30 minutes so you're actually comfortable.

Step Four: The Microneedling Treatment, which takes 15 to 20 minutes.

This is the main event. Once you're numb, here's what happens:

We wipe off the numbing cream and apply a hyaluronic acid serum to your face. This keeps your skin hydrated during treatment and helps the needles glide smoothly.

Then, we use a medical-grade microneedling pen—a small device with a tip that has tiny needles, 0.5 millimeters to 2.5 millimeters deep, depending on your scar depth.

The process: We pass the pen over your skin in sections: forehead, cheeks, chin, nose, and so on. The needles vibrate rapidly, creating thousands of tiny punctures. Each section takes 2 to 3 passes. Full face: 15 to 20 minutes total.

What it feels like: With numbing, it's scratching, light pressure, tingling. Some areas like cheeks and forehead feel like nothing. Others like nose and upper lip are slightly more sensitive. The sound is a light buzzing or vibrating sound. There's no bleeding, just tiny pinprick dots that fade quickly.

Needle depth varies: Shallow concerns like texture or pores use 0.5 to 1.0 millimeters. Moderate scars use 1.5 to 2.0 millimeters. Deep scars use 2.0 to 2.5 millimeters, which is medical-grade only and requires an expert technician.

Deeper needles equal more collagen stimulation, which equals better results for deep scars. But also slightly more sensation and longer recovery.

Step Five: Post-Treatment Serum and Soothing, which takes 5 minutes.

Right after microneedling, your skin has thousands of micro-channels open. This is the PERFECT time to apply serums—they absorb 10 times better than normal.

We apply: A calming serum with peptides, growth factors, or hyaluronic acid. Optional cooling mask, which reduces redness and feels amazing.

If you added PRP, we apply it now. Your own blood plasma soaks into those micro-channels and supercharges collagen production.

What your skin looks like right now: RED, like a sunburn. Warm to the touch. Tiny pinprick dots visible that fade within hours. Slight puffiness that goes away overnight.

Step Six: Aftercare Instructions. Critical—Don't Skip This.

This is where most people mess up. Your skin is VULNERABLE for the next 48 hours. Follow these rules, or you risk infection, breakouts, or delayed healing.

First 24 Hours, which is the CRITICAL WINDOW:

DO NOT: Touch your face with dirty hands. Wear makeup. Exercise or sweat—it raises body temp and can cause irritation. Use hot water—lukewarm only. Go in the sun—UV exposure equals hyperpigmentation risk. Use ANY active skincare like retinol, acids, vitamin C, or exfoliants.

DO: Gently cleanse with a gentle, fragrance-free cleanser like Cerave or Cetaphil. Apply a simple moisturizer that's fragrance-free with no actives. Sleep on a clean pillowcase.

Days 2 to 7:

DO: Use SPF 50+ sunscreen DAILY—even indoors, even in Edmonton winters. UV can cause dark spots on healing skin. Keep skin hydrated with hyaluronic acid serum plus gentle moisturizer. Let peeling happen naturally—do NOT pick, scrub, or exfoliate.

AVOID: Retinoids, acids, exfoliants for 7 days. Gym or sweating for 48 hours. Makeup for 48 hours, or use clean, mineral makeup only after day 2.

Why does this matter SO much?

Your skin has micro-channels for 24 to 48 hours. Bacteria, dirt, or harsh products can get IN and cause: Infection. Breakouts. Hyperpigmentation or dark spots. Delayed healing.

Follow aftercare equals smooth recovery and great results. Ignore aftercare equals problems.

Step Seven: Recovery Timeline. What Happens in the Weeks After.

Here's what to expect day by day:

Hour 1 to 6 on the day of treatment: Face is RED and warm, like a sunburn. Mild swelling is normal. No pain, just tightness.

Day 1, the next morning: Redness starts fading—still pink, but not as intense. Skin feels tight and dry. Swelling should be gone.

Days 2 to 3: Skin may feel rough or sandpapery as dead cells prepare to shed. Light flaking starts. You CAN wear makeup from day 2 to 3—mineral makeup preferred.

Days 4 to 7: Peeling and flaking. This is GOOD—old, damaged skin shedding. Redness mostly gone. Skin looks normal to others. You'll notice texture changes they won't.

Week 2 to 4: Skin looks completely normal. Texture starts improving—feels smoother.

Week 4 to 6: This is when the magic happens. New collagen has formed. Scars look shallower, skin feels firmer and smoother.

Social downtime: 2 to 3 days. You CAN go out, but your skin will be visibly pink for 48 hours.

Step Eight: When Do You See Results? The Waiting Game.

Here's what surprises first-timers: Microneedling results are NOT instant.

When you leave the clinic, you won't look better—you'll look red and irritated. But here's what's happening under the surface:

Days 1 to 7: Your body is healing the micro-injuries. Weeks 2 to 4: Collagen production ramps up. Weeks 4 to 6: New collagen fills in scars and smooths texture.

Results timeline by session: Session 1, skin texture feels smoother, which is subtle. Session 2 to 3, scars start looking shallower, 20 to 30 percent improvement. Session 4 to 5, noticeable improvement, 50 to 60 percent improvement. Session 6, maximum results, 70 to 80 percent improvement for most clients.

Each session builds on the last. This is cumulative—session 6 looks better than session 1 because you have MORE new collagen.

What if you hate it? The Safety Net.

Let's address the fear: What if it makes my skin worse?

The truth: Microneedling is one of the safest skin treatments when done properly. Serious complications are VERY rare.

Possible normal reactions: Redness for 2 to 3 days—this is expected. Dryness and flaking—also expected, old skin shedding. Mild breakout—if you have acne-prone skin, purging can happen and clears up within a week.

Serious complications, which are rare but possible if aftercare is ignored: Infection if you touch your face with dirty hands or don't cleanse properly. Hyperpigmentation if you go in the sun without SPF.

At Oxyderm, we minimize risks by: Using sterile, single-use needle cartridges—no cross-contamination. Giving you detailed aftercare instructions. Offering a follow-up check-in if you have concerns.

And here's the best part: Microneedling doesn't REMOVE skin like laser or chemical peels. It just stimulates your body's natural healing. If you don't love the results after 1 to 2 sessions, you can stop. Your skin goes back to baseline—it won't be worse than before.
