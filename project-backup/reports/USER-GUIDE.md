## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard has **7 main tabs** at the top. Tap any tab to switch views.

### Tab 1: Schedule 📅

**What it shows:** Today's appointments with times, client names, services.

**What you can do:**
- See who is coming and when
- Tap on any appointment to see full details (phone, email, notes)
- Click "Mark Done" when you finish with a client

**Example:**
```
11:30 AM  Harpreet Saini       Treatment
12:00 PM  Morgan Bodnaruk      Treatment
1:00 PM   Karuna Arora         Treatment
```

**Updated automatically** from Square each morning at 7am.

---

### Tab 2: Financials 💰

**What it shows:** Money coming in and going out.

**Sections:**
- **Stats:** Total revenue, expenses, balance
- **Pre-Auth:** Payments you've collected (advance payments)
- **Expenses:** Supplies, rent, wages breakdown

**What you can do:**
- Add a new expense (Date, Category, Amount, Notes)
- Mark pre-auth as collected when received
- Export monthly report

**Example Expenses:**
- Supplies: Laser cartridges, gel, sterilization
- Rent: Clinic space monthly
- Staff: Wages for assistants
- Marketing: Ad spend

---

### Tab 3: Content Review 📸

**What it shows:** Photos and videos ready for social media (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook).

**What you can do:**
- Review before/after photos
- Watch treatment videos
- Add captions and hashtags
- Schedule posts to social media
- Check what's already posted

**Video Quality:**
- All videos broadcast quality (19 Mbps)
- Color graded and audio normalized
- Closed captions included

**Photos:**
- High resolution (for print and web)
- Organized by treatment type (LHR, Microneedling)

---

### Tab 4: Calendar 📆

**What it shows:** Full week view of appointments and availability.

**What you can do:**
- Tap any day to see all appointments
- Tap "Book" to add a new client
- Drag appointments to reschedule (if needed)
- See availability at a glance

**Color coding:**
- Green = Confirmed
- Yellow = Pending confirmation
- Red = Cancelled

**Next week availability:**
- Opens automatically after last appointment closes
- Clients can book via link in footer

---

### Tab 5: Video Editor 🎬

**What it shows:** Treatment videos in various stages.

**What you can do:**
- Review treatment footage
- Watch edited versions (color graded, captioned)
- Download for social media
- Create retargeting cuts (short 15s-60s versions for ads)

**Video Types:**
- **Master:** Full treatment walkthrough (broadcast quality)
- **TOFU:** Educational (120 sec) — problem awareness
- **MOFU:** Results focused (60 sec) — before/after + client voice
- **BOFU:** Direct offer (30-45 sec) — booking CTA

**For Social Media:**
- Instagram Reels: 15-60 seconds
- TikTok: 15-60 seconds
- Facebook: 60-120 seconds
- YouTube: Full video

---

### Tab 6: Agents 🤖

**What it shows:** 6 AI agents that help with different tasks.

**The 6 Agents:**

1. **📅 Scheduler** — Calendars, booking times, posting schedule
   - Ask: "What's our posting schedule?" or "When can I book a new client?"

2. **🎬 Video Editor** — Scripts, scenes, thumbnails, video quality
   - Ask: "How many videos do we have?" or "What are the specs for our master video?"

3. **📣 Social Media** — Copy, captions, hashtags, platforms
   - Ask: "Write a hook for LHR" or "Best hashtags for Edmonton?"

4. **💰 Finance** — Revenue, expenses, margins, profit tracking
   - Ask: "How much did we make today?" or "What's our membership price?"

5. **👥 Client Care** — Follow-up scripts, reviews, rebooking
   - Ask: "What's the 24-hour follow-up script?" or "How do we ask for reviews?"

6. **🔍 Research** — Competitors, SEO, keywords, market trends
   - Ask: "Who are our competitors?" or "Best keywords for Edmonton?"

**How to use:**
1. Tap an agent card to open chat
2. Type your question or ask via voice
3. Agent responds with learned knowledge or real-time info
4. Your questions help agents learn (saved to memory)

### Tab 7: Sarah 🎤 (Voice Assistant)

**What it shows:** Your personal AI assistant for questions about clients, appointments, and services.

**Sarah Knows:**
- Today's appointment schedule
- Client names, phone numbers, emails
- Treatment types and areas
- Payment status and balances
- Visit counts (how many times client has been seen)
- Services offered (Laser Hair Removal, Microneedling)

**How to Ask Questions:**

**Example Questions Sarah Can Answer:**

1. **About a specific client:**
   - "What time is Harpreet coming?" → "11:30 AM"
   - "What's Mehak's phone number?" → Shows phone
   - "Notes for Morgan?" → Shows any special notes
   - "How many times has Alexandra visited?" → "7th visit"

2. **About today's schedule:**
   - "Who's coming today?" → List of all clients
   - "How many appointments?" → Number + details
   - "Who is next?" → Next appointment in 5 mins or more

3. **About payment:**
   - "Did Harpreet pay?" → "See Square"
   - "Who owes money?" → Outstanding balances
   - "What's the balance for Mehak?" → Specific amount

4. **New capability: Web Search**
   - "What is laser hair removal?" → Searches the web, answers
   - "How long does microneedling take to work?" → Web results
   - "Is laser safe for sensitive skin?" → Web answer + local context

**How to Use Sarah:**

**Option 1: Voice (Easiest)**
1. Tap the microphone button 🎤 (in the Sarah section)
2. Speak your question clearly
3. Sarah listens and answers aloud
4. Tap again to listen again

**Option 2: Typing**
1. Type your question in the text box
2. Tap "Send" or press Enter
3. Sarah responds (voice + text)

**Example Conversation:**
```
You: "Who is next?"
Sarah: "Next: Morgan at 12:00 PM. Treatment, 60 min session."

You: "What's Morgan's phone?"
Sarah: "(587) 343-4443"

You: "How much did she pay?"
Sarah: "Morgan: See Square."
```

**Tips for Best Results:**
- Use client's first or last name for personal questions
- Be specific: "Who is at 1pm" instead of "Who is coming"
- Sarah learns over time — questions you ask frequently get faster answers

## Troubleshooting

### "Sarah won't listen to me"

**Solutions:**
1. Make sure you're on HTTPS (Safari or Chrome)
2. Check browser microphone permission (Settings → Privacy)
3. Try typing instead (tap text box below mic)
4. Refresh page if microphone was used in another tab

### "Appointments not loading"

**Solutions:**
1. Refresh page (Cmd+R or Ctrl+R)
2. Check your internet connection
3. Wait 5 minutes (Square syncs at top of hour)
4. Check Schedule tab (may have loaded already)

### "Sarah doesn't know an answer"

**Solutions:**
1. Try different wording: "Harpreet's phone" instead of "Call Harpreet"
2. Sarah can web search: "How long does laser take?" will search the web
3. Check Schedule tab directly if it's about appointments

### "Web search not working for Sarah"

**Solutions:**
1. Verify HTTPS connection (not HTTP)
2. Check browser has internet
3. Try a different question
4. DuckDuckGo may timeout (try again in 10 sec)

### "Agents not learning"

**Normal behavior** — agents learn gradually.
- Your questions are saved automatically
- Agent memory updated weekly as patterns emerge
- Check agent chat history (scroll up) to see what they know

## Tips & Best Practices

### Sarah Voice Assistant

- **Speak clearly** when using microphone (Sarah understands most accents)
- **Use first or last names** for client questions (e.g., "Harpreet" not "the client at 11:30")
- **Be specific** about what you need (e.g., "phone" instead of "info")
- **Save web answers** that are useful — Sarah remembers common questions

### Daily Workflow

1. **Morning:** Open Schedule → Review day
2. **Throughout day:** Tap appointment → Mark Done when finished
3. **Social media:** Content Review → Schedule posts (pre-scheduled 1-2 posts/day)
4. **Before leaving:** Financials → Add expenses → Export report

### Weekly Workflow

1. **Monday:** Review last week's revenue (Financials tab)
2. **Wednesday:** Plan week's content (Content Review + Agents)
3. **Friday:** Schedule next week's social posts
4. **Sunday:** Backup database (automatic)
